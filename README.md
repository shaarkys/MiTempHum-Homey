App for Xiaomi Temperature and Humidity Monitor Clock LYWSD02MM, Xiaomi Mijia LYWSD03MMC Temperature and Humidity, Qingping CGD1, Xiaomi XMWSDJ04MMC, and Xiaomi Smart Temperature and Humidity Monitor 3 (MJWSD05MMC) Bluetooth temperature and humidity sensors.

MJWSD05MMC with stock Xiaomi firmware uses encrypted Bluetooth advertisements. Select its dedicated driver, then enter the sensor's 32-character hexadecimal BLE bindkey in Advanced Settings. Each sensor needs its own key. The driver reads temperature, humidity, battery, and signal strength without connecting to the sensor; it does not synchronise its clock. See [setup and testing](docs/MJWSD05MMC.md).
